The truest measure

Namit sir

The truest measure of your life's work is not the trillion, the Oscars on the shelf or the headlines in the archive. It is the young filmmaker in Chennai who sat in a theatre that Diwali of 2026 and thought, for the first time, without hesitation: my story can be the world's story.

The garage and the cathedral

It did not stay a garage.

It became a cathedral, built not of stone but of light and imagination, and of the stubborn, magnificent refusal to accept that India's place in global cinema was anything less than first.
